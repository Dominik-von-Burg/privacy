Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Datacenter Siege ("the app") is an offline strategy game in which you build an army and raid data centers. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data Collection

The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All gameplay (army composition, currency, battle outcomes, progress) is generated and processed entirely on your device.

No Network Access

The app does not require internet connectivity and makes no network requests. No analytics, advertising SDKs, in-app purchases, or third-party trackers are included.

Local Storage

Your game progress (army, currency, base integrity, and current tier) is saved only to local storage on your device so you can pick up where you left off. Nothing is written to any server. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Children's Privacy

The app is a general-audience game and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Changes

If the app ever adds features that require data collection or network access, this policy will be updated before release.
